Privacy Policy - Bump Detector Bot

Last Updated: May 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the Bump Detector Discord Bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects any information obtained through its operation. By using the Bot, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot may collect limited information necessary for its functionality, such as:

Discord server IDs and role IDs to manage bump notifications and customizable role pinging.

Basic metadata related to bump events (e.g., timestamps of bumps).

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or other personally identifiable data unless explicitly provided by you for support purposes.

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used solely to:

Facilitate the Bot’s core functionality, including detecting server bumps and pinging the designated role.

Ensure proper operation and troubleshoot issues within Discord servers where the Bot is active.

We do not use the collected information for advertising, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to the Bot’s functionality.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data collected by the Bot to third parties, except:

When required by law or to comply with legal processes.

To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Bot, its developers, or users.

Data may be stored on secure servers hosted by third-party providers, but only for operational purposes and in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect the data collected by the Bot from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Data is stored only for as long as necessary to provide the Bot’s services or as required by law.

If you remove the Bot from your Discord server, any associated data (e.g., server IDs, role IDs) will be deleted within a reasonable timeframe.

5. User Rights

You have the right to:

Request information about the data collected by the Bot for your Discord server.

Request the deletion of any data associated with your server by removing the Bot and contacting us.

Contact us for any privacy-related concerns or inquiries.

6. Compliance with Discord Policies

The Bot operates in compliance with Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

We ensure that data collection and usage practices align with Discord’s requirements for bots.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting the revised version on our website or within the Bot’s documentation.

Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

8. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the Bot’s data practices, please contact us via Discord at our official Discord contact.

By using the Bump Detector Bot, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy.
